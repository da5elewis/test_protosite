Key Learnings

View all →
01

Context is everything

The quality of AI output is almost entirely determined by the context you provide — background, audience, constraints, tone. Generic prompts return generic output.

02

Model choice should match the task

Different models have meaningfully different strengths. Claude tends toward nuance and caution; GPT-4o is more decisive and action-oriented. Neither is universally better.

03

Treat prompts like hypotheses

The best results come from iteration, not inspiration. Write a prompt, evaluate the output critically, and refine. This is the same discipline as any good strategy work.

04

AI multiplies, not replaces

The sharpest outputs come when you bring domain knowledge and judgment, then use AI as a force multiplier — not a substitute for thinking.
